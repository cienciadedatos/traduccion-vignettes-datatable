msgid \"\" \"In the broadest sense, `.SD` is just shorthand for capturing a variable that\" \" comes up frequently in the context of data analysis. That \" \"is, `.SD` is in its most basic guise a *reflexive reference* to the \" \"`data.table` itself -- as we'll see in examples below, this is particularly \" \"helpful for chaining together \"queries\" (extractions/subsets/etc using \" \"`[`).
